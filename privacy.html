Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 14, 2026

At 927 Technologies, we respect your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle any information you provide when using our website (https://927technologies.netlify.app).

Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal data automatically. Our website does not use cookies, tracking scripts, or analytics tools that store information about your visit.

The only information we ever receive is what you voluntarily choose to send us through our contact form or direct email. This may include:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your phone number (optional)
  • Details about your project or enquiry

How We Use Your Information

Any information you provide is used exclusively to respond to your enquiry and discuss potential web development services. We will never:

  • Sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.
  • Use your information for marketing purposes without your explicit consent.
  • Add you to any mailing list without your permission.

Data Retention

If you contact us, we may retain your email correspondence for reference purposes. If you would like us to delete your information, simply request it by email and we will remove it promptly.

Third-Party Services

Our website does not integrate with any third-party services that collect personal data (such as Google Analytics, social media trackers, or advertising networks).

Our contact form is powered by Formspree, which processes form submissions on our behalf. Formspree's privacy policy can be found at https://formspree.io/legal/privacy-policy/.

Links to Other Websites

Our portfolio and work pages may contain links to external websites (client projects). We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those third-party sites.

Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed at individuals under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
